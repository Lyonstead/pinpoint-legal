# Pinpoint privacy policy

**Last updated:** 29 September 2026

Pinpoint is a golf improvement app made by Lacuna Technology ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: tommylyons02@gmail.com.

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your email address and a password (stored by our sign-in provider, never by us in readable form). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google.

**Your golf.** What you enter: your name as you want it shown, handicap and goal (and each change you make to your handicap, with its date), how often you play and practise, where you can practise, the rounds you log (course name, the tees you played with their rating and slope, hole scores, putts and the optional details you add) and the lessons you complete with your scores and feedback. We also keep a record of which lessons the app recommended to you and whether you started or finished them, so we can improve the recommendations.

**Approximate location, only when you start a round.** To list nearby golf courses, the app asks for your approximate location. It is rounded to about 1 km before it leaves your phone, is sent only to our course data provider to find courses, and is not stored by us.

**Usage data and crash reports, only if you agree.** The app asks before sending any. If you say yes, it sends events such as "round completed" or "lesson finished", counts (like how many holes had extra detail), your app version and device type, and crash reports. These are linked to a random account id, never to your name, email, course names or scores. You can turn this off at any time in Profile → Privacy.

We don't collect precise location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and we don't show ads.

## Why

- To run the app: keep your rounds and lessons, back them up to your account and sync them between your devices.
- To recommend practice, which is the point of the app.
- With your consent, to understand how the app is used and fix crashes.

Our legal bases are performing our agreement with you (running the app) and, for usage data and crash reports, your consent.

## Who processes it

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. It is processed for us by:

- **Supabase**: sign-in and the database that backs up your account. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
- **Google**: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
- **GolfCore** (golfcore.org): receives your approximate location to find nearby courses.
- **PostHog** (EU hosting): usage data and crash reports, only if you agreed.

## How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, your account and golf data are deleted straight away; if you ask us by email, we do it within 30 days. Copies in our provider's backups are gone within 30 days. Usage data is kept for 12 months.

## Your rights

You can see and change most of your data in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, or to restrict or object to how we use it, by emailing tommylyons02@gmail.com. To delete your account: in the app, Profile → Delete account, or see https://lyonstead.github.io/pinpoint-legal/delete-account. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

## Children

Pinpoint isn't intended for children under 16.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
