# Delete your Pinpoint account

## In the app

Open Pinpoint, tap your profile picture on Home, then **Delete account** at the bottom of your profile, and confirm.

## Without the app

Email tommylyons02@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What's deleted

Everything: your account and email address, your profile and handicap history, every round you logged, your lesson history and scores, and the record of lessons recommended to you. Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from anonymous usage data if you had chosen to share it, which isn't linked to your name or email and is removed after 12 months.
